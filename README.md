A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Sunday, 22 November
2026, entered in [make.fun's "Sum up 22 November's world news in one
image" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122), $100 shared by
the top 50.

The front page carries six stories of the day: tankers struck near the
Strait of Hormuz as Iran claims it destroyed US Patriot radars in Bahrain;
Russian missiles landing in Poland; COP31 deadlocked in overtime over
fossil fuels; Trump, Xi and Putin meeting in Shenzhen; "El Chapo Isidro"
captured in Mexico; and the AI sell-off pushing US stocks into correction.
The hero photo is an AI image of a burning tanker at dusk; every word on
the page is drawn with PIL, so the text is exactly as written.

`main.py` copies `news.png` to `/output/entry.png` — no network, no clock,
so two runs write identical bytes.
